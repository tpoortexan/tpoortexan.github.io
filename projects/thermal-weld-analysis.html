Thermal Analysis & Weld Optimization

Heat Transfer · Thermodynamics · Root-Cause Analysis

Heat TransferThermodynamicsRoot-Cause AnalysisProblem Solving

Overview

I supported an engineer investigating overheating of a temperature-sensitive component during the welding process. The problem required determining why heat from the welding operation was affecting the component and identifying potential design or process changes to reduce the thermal impact.

My Contributions

  • Investigated the thermal behavior of the welding process.
  • Used principles of heat transfer and thermodynamics to approximate heat penetration into the component.
  • Assisted with identifying the likely causes of excessive temperature exposure.
  • Developed engineering-based recommendations for changes to the design and process.
  • Communicated findings and recommendations to the responsible engineer.

Engineering Approach

The analysis involved simplifying the physical problem into an engineering model and estimating how heat could propagate through the component during welding. The goal was not simply to calculate a temperature, but to answer a practical engineering question:

How can the design or process be modified to prevent a temperature-sensitive region from being exposed to excessive heat?
