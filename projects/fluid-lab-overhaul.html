Overview

The FLO Senior Design project involved the overhaul and development of modules for a fluid mechanics laboratory. The project required balancing engineering performance with practical considerations such as student use, instructor requirements, manufacturability, and system operation.

My Contributions

  • Researched head-loss behavior through piping and flow-conditioning components.
  • Investigated methods for normalizing the velocity gradient of flow through a pipe.
  • Compared the behavior and application of honeycomb flow straighteners and tube bundles.
  • Performed initial head-loss calculations using the Darcy-Weisbach equation.
  • Used the Hazen-Williams equation as an additional method of evaluating head loss.
  • Iterated the design of the head-loss module based on engineering calculations and team feedback.
  • Worked with a third-party drafter to develop and revise a professional P&ID.
  • Incorporated feedback from team leadership concerning student and professor use into the final design.

Engineering Analysis

A primary portion of my work involved estimating frictional head loss through the system using the Darcy-Weisbach relationship:

h_f = f · (L / D) · (V² / 2g)

where hf is frictional head loss, f the Darcy friction factor, L the pipe length, D the hydraulic diameter, V the average fluid velocity, and g gravitational acceleration. The analysis was used to evaluate the effect of different design configurations and to help guide development of the final module.
