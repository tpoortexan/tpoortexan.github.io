## Featured Projects

### 1. Fluid Laboratory Module — Senior Design

**Role:** Mechanical Engineering Team Member  
**Project:** FLO — Fluid Lab Overhaul  
**Focus:** Fluid Mechanics • Head Loss • Flow Conditioning • Mechanical Design • P&ID

The FLO Senior Design project involved the overhaul and development of modules for a fluid mechanics laboratory. The project required balancing engineering performance with practical considerations such as student use, instructor requirements, manufacturability, and system operation.

#### My Contributions

- Researched head-loss behavior through piping and flow-conditioning components.
- Investigated methods for normalizing the velocity gradient of flow through a pipe.
- Compared the behavior and application of **honeycomb flow straighteners and tube bundles**.
- Performed initial head-loss calculations using the **Darcy-Weisbach equation**.
- Used the **Hazen-Williams equation** as an additional method of evaluating head loss.
- Iterated the design of the head-loss module based on engineering calculations and team feedback.
- Worked with a third-party drafter to develop and revise a professional **P&ID**.
- Incorporated feedback from team leadership concerning student and professor use into the final design.

#### Engineering Analysis

A primary portion of my work involved estimating head loss through the system.

The Darcy-Weisbach relationship was used to estimate frictional head loss:

$$
h_f = f \frac{L}{D}\frac{V^2}{2g}
$$

where:

- $h_f$ = frictional head loss
- $f$ = Darcy friction factor
- $L$ = pipe length
- $D$ = hydraulic diameter
- $V$ = average fluid velocity
- $g$ = gravitational acceleration

The analysis was used to evaluate the effect of different design configurations and to help guide the development of the final module.

# Engineering Design Process

I approach mechanical engineering problems using a structured process:

### 01 — Define

Understand the problem, requirements, constraints, and desired outcome.

### 02 — Research

Identify relevant engineering principles, standards, existing designs, materials, manufacturing processes, and available solutions.

### 03 — Develop

Generate concepts and identify practical design alternatives.

### 04 — Analyze

Use engineering calculations, modeling, simulation, or experimental data to evaluate the proposed solution.

### 05 — Design

Develop the mechanical design while considering performance, manufacturability, cost, reliability, and usability.

### 06 — Test

Use physical testing and measured data to validate assumptions and evaluate performance.

### 07 — Iterate

Identify deficiencies and modify the design or process based on engineering evidence.

### 08 — Document

Communicate the final design and engineering decisions through drawings, calculations, reports, diagrams, and other technical documentation.
