Overview

I supported engineering work involving the optimization of a Joyal Lug Fuser manufacturing process. The objective was to improve the consistency and quality of fused lugs by evaluating process parameters and using test data to identify improved operating conditions.

My Contributions

  • Assisted with collecting experimental process data.
  • Evaluated the relationship between process parameters and resulting product quality.
  • Worked with engineering personnel to identify appropriate operating conditions.
  • Used collected data to help optimize current and compression settings.
  • Contributed to improving process consistency and quality.

Engineering Approach

The project followed an iterative test-and-evaluate approach:

  1. 01Process Parameters
  2. 02Test
  3. 03Collect Data
  4. 04Analyze
  5. 05Adjust
  6. 06Retest

↻ Repeat until process consistency and quality are achieved

This provided practical experience applying engineering analysis to a manufacturing environment where process consistency and repeatability were important.
