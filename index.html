Engineering Design Process

A structured approach I use to work through mechanical engineering problems.

01

Define

Understand the problem, requirements, constraints, and desired outcome.

02

Research

Identify relevant principles, standards, existing designs, materials, and processes.

03

Develop

Generate concepts and identify practical design alternatives.

04

Analyze

Use calculations, modeling, simulation, or data to evaluate the solution.

05

Design

Develop the design considering performance, manufacturability, cost, and usability.

06

Test

Use physical testing and measured data to validate assumptions and performance.

07

Iterate

Identify deficiencies and refine the design based on engineering evidence.

08

Document

Communicate decisions through drawings, calculations, reports, and diagrams.
